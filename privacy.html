THE GILDED ACE

PRIVACY POLICY

Effective: September 10, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes information handled when you use The Gilded Ace website and account features.

2. Information You Provide

Account features may process information you provide, such as an email address, display name, authentication credentials handled by the authentication provider, and account settings.

3. Game and Account Information

The service may store fictional Ace Credit balances, wins, losses, games played, game-specific statistics, virtual collection items, poker/table state, account identifiers, and related gameplay information needed to operate the service.

4. Browser Storage

The Gilded Ace uses browser storage such as localStorage and sessionStorage for functions including local profile/game state, recent game history and the session-based 18+ confirmation. Clearing browser data may remove locally stored information.

5. Service Providers

The service currently uses Supabase for account authentication and cloud-backed profile/game data and GitHub Pages for website hosting. Those providers may process technical information according to their own terms and privacy practices. Do not place secret/service-role database keys in public browser code.

6. How Information Is Used

  • Provide accounts and authenticate users.
  • Maintain fictional balances, statistics and virtual collections.
  • Operate multiplayer and game features.
  • Maintain security, diagnose errors and prevent abuse.
  • Improve and administer the service.

7. Children

The Gilded Ace is intended for users age 18 and older and is not directed to children under 13. Users who are under 18 should not create an account or use the service. If the operator learns that personal information was collected from a child under 13 in circumstances covered by applicable law, the operator should take appropriate steps to stop collection and delete the information as required.

8. Sale of Personal Information

The Gilded Ace does not state or intend that user personal information is sold to advertisers. If advertising, analytics, tracking or data-sharing practices are added later, this Privacy Policy must be reviewed and updated before deployment.

9. Security

Reasonable technical measures should be used to protect account information, including Supabase Row Level Security for user data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Data Retention and Account Requests

Information should be kept only as long as reasonably needed to operate the service, meet security needs and satisfy applicable obligations. Users may contact the site administrator to request access, correction or deletion of account information where applicable. The operator should verify the requester before changing or disclosing account data.

11. Third-Party Links

The site may link to third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

12. Changes

This policy may be updated when features or data practices change. The effective date above should be updated when material changes are published.

13. Contact

Privacy questions or account-data requests should be directed to the administrator of The Gilded Ace through the contact method published by the site operator.

Operator note: Before launch, add a reliable public contact method for privacy/account requests. If you later add analytics, advertising, payment processing, purchased credits, prizes, or new third-party services, this policy must be reviewed and updated.
